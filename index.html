STEP - 3: BEST AI TOOLS

Best AI Tools For YouTube Creators 2026

Ye tools aapka poora content creation process automate kar denge.

ChatGPT / Claude

Complete YouTube script, title, description aur tags generate karein. Free plan se shuru karein.

ElevenLabs

AI Voiceover: Ekdum natural Hindi/English voice generate karo. Free plan mein 10,000 characters/month.

CapCut AI

Video Editor: Auto captions, background removal, transitions — sab kuch automatic. Bilkul Free hai.

Canva AI

Thumbnails: Professional YouTube thumbnails minutes mein Magic Design feature se AI suggest karta hai.

Animaker / Vyond

Bina camera ke animated educational videos banao. Face show karne ki zaroorat nahi.

VidIQ / TubeBuddy

Trending topics, keywords aur competitor analysis. Free plan bahut kuch deta hai.

Midjourney / Firefly

AI Images: Thumbnail ke liye unique AI-generated Images banao. Copyright-Free hote hain.

Google Trends + Gemini

Topic Research: Trending topics real-time dekhna aur Gemini se unpar content ideas generate karna.
